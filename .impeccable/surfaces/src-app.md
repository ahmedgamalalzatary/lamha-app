Scope: whole Lamha demo (customer, partner, rider apps). Mode: Operate. Code-led (no image generation available).

Audience: Fayoum customers on Android; partners; riders; the client deciding the deal. Job: order with total trust. Constraint: mobile column only, Arabic RTL, static data.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Lamha is the honest bill. Every price, fee, change, cancel and refund is written on a live receipt the customer can read line by line; the category default (orange photo-card feed with fees hidden until checkout) is refused.

OWN-WORLD: Ballpoint-ink blue shell (#1C2FA8 family) over cool white paper; receipt sheets with a zigzag torn edge and dotted leaders; Ruqaa handwriting (Aref Ruqaa) only for stamps and the wordmark; Readex Pro with tabular numerals for everything else; stamp green for approved/paid, stamp red for void/refund, highlighter yellow only on the total. States are stamped, never deleted: a cancelled line is struck and stamped.

STORY: The customer sees what they'll pay before they commit, watches the receipt get stamped as the order moves, and can cancel, edit, or get refunded from that same receipt.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: blue ink header (wordmark, address chip, search), service row (restaurants, mart, pharmacy, send anything), and when an order is live, a receipt stub pinned at top showing its current stamp and ETA; then reorder rail and stores, each with its real delivery fee printed. Primary action: search or a service.

FORM: Grounded candidate #1 (itemized receipt), the pick card over assigned #4 (khayamiya); seed key 8c1ba362. Raises: from split-flap, ETA digits flip on change; from labanotation, the ETA bar's length is the exact remaining time; from ticket wallet, nothing disappears, it gets stamped; from Crouwel, every receipt column sits on one shared grid.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
